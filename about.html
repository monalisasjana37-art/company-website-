About Monalisa Electro Make

We manufacture electrical equipment and carry out complete electrical installation work, so customers can order products and get a site wired by one team.

Monalisa Electro Make makes bus bars, cable boxes, transformer enclosures and lighting, built to standard sizes or to a customer's drawing. The same team also takes on full electrical projects on site: wiring, panels, lighting and power connections for hotels, offices, factories and showrooms.

Because manufacturing and site work sit under one roof, a project can use our own bus bars, cable boxes and lighting without waiting on a separate supplier, and a product order can be followed by installation help if the customer needs it.
